2026 Hybrid Cloud Security Survey: Exposing the AI Security Illusion

New global research reveals a critical disconnect between AI security confidence and reality in hybrid cloud environments. Based on a survey of cybersecurity leaders worldwide.

Overconfidence Gap

78% of organizations believe their AI security is effective, yet only 23% have experienced a real AI-powered threat — revealing a dangerous gap between perception and preparedness.

Visibility Blind Spots

64% of security leaders admit they lack complete visibility into their hybrid cloud environments, making AI-driven threat detection significantly less effective than assumed.

Skills & Strategy Gap

Only 31% of organizations have dedicated AI security expertise on staff, while 67% report that existing security teams lack the training to manage AI-specific risks.
