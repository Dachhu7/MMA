Global Surge in Ransomware Attacks Targeting SMEs

A sharp 38% increase in ransomware attacks has been recorded globally, with small and mid-sized enterprises emerging as primary victims. Attackers are leveraging automation, AI-generated phishing kits, and exposed cloud configurations to breach networks faster than ever.

Security experts emphasize continuous patching, Zero-Trust architecture, multi-factor authentication, and real-time threat monitoring as critical countermeasures in today’s evolving threat landscape.

Enterprise Security

28 February 2026

Browser-Based Attacks Becoming the #1 Enterprise Risk

Modern browsers have become the new enterprise perimeter. Over 90% of breaches now begin with phishing campaigns, malicious extensions, or compromised SaaS sessions.

Organizations are deploying browser isolation, AI-driven anomaly detection, and endpoint response systems to defend against credential theft and session hijacking attacks.

Red Team vs Blue Team: The Growing Cybersecurity Talent Gap

With the cybersecurity workforce shortage exceeding 3 million globally, companies are investing in red team simulations and blue team defense programs to proactively detect vulnerabilities.

Ethical hackers are identifying zero-day exploits before malicious actors, while SOC teams are strengthening real-time detection and response capabilities.
