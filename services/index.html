Security Solutions We Deliver

Attack Surface Visibility

We provide comprehensive visibility into your attack surface and incident response preparedness by penetrating exploitable vulnerabilities and simulating real-world attack paths.

Using a combination of Red and Purple Team testing, we validate security controls, detection capabilities, and response effectiveness.

Red Teaming Assessment

Our Red Team engagements emulate real-world adversaries using proven tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs).

This measures the effectiveness of people, processes, and technology used to defend enterprise environments.

Managed Detection & Response (MDR)

Our MDR services ingest multi-signal telemetry into our XDR platform to detect attacks within seconds.

We contain attacks within 15 minutes and suppress them within 4 hours — a unique, outcome-driven offering.

Threat Intelligence & Analytics

Accelerate enterprise security with a virtual Cyber Fusion Center that powers advanced threat intelligence and analytics.

Enables faster detection, improved correlation, and proactive defense against emerging threats.

Vulnerability Assessment

Cyber resilience begins with visibility. Our Vulnerability Assessment services help organizations proactively identify security weaknesses, assess risk, and prioritize remediation before they can be exploited.

Through continuous assessments and actionable insights, we have helped our customers strengthen their security posture and stay ahead of evolving threats.

Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

Regulatory compliance is more than meeting standards—it’s about building trust. MMA Infosec helps organizations establish effective governance frameworks, manage cyber risk, and achieve compliance with industry and regulatory requirements.

We enable organizations to operate with confidence in an increasingly complex digital landscape through practical governance, risk management, and compliance-driven security strategies.
