Industries

We Understand Every industry has something it cannot afford to lose

For a financial institution, it is trust.
For a hospital, it is continuity of care.
For an airport, it is uninterrupted operations.

At MMA Infosec, we begin by understanding what must remain protected — and what must continue running — before recommending any security control.

Finance

Because trust moves at transaction speed.

We help financial organizations protect customer information, digital services, applications and identities while maintaining the availability and integrity expected from critical financial platforms.

Real Estate

Because modern buildings are also connected environments.

From corporate networks and cloud platforms to customer portals and smart-building systems, we help protect the technologies that keep properties, people and services connected.

Healthcare

Because security should never interrupt patient care.

We support healthcare organizations in strengthening the security of clinical systems, endpoints, applications and sensitive information without creating unnecessary disruption to essential services.

Hospitality

Because the guest experience depends on systems guests never see.

Booking platforms, payment systems, wireless networks and third-party integrations all operate behind the scenes. We help secure these environments while keeping the customer experience seamless.

Critical Infrastructure

Because security controls must protect operations — not slow them down.

Our experience across IT and OT environments includes asset visibility, threat detection, risk management, incident readiness and security architecture for live operational environments.

Aviation

Because an airport cannot pause for cybersecurity.

We support aviation cybersecurity initiatives across enterprise IT and operational technology, with a strong focus on visibility, resilience and implementing controls without disrupting ongoing airport operations.
