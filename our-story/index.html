Our Story

From a vision born in 2020 to becoming one of UAE's most trusted cybersecurity partners — built on expertise, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to customer success.

Our Beginning
2020

Founded in Trust

Founded in 2020 in UAE, MMA Infosec was built on a simple yet powerful belief: customers deserve a trusted partner who puts their success first.

With over 25 years of leadership experience in the technology industry, our Founder, Amit Kumar, envisioned a company that would do more than just providing IT, OT and cybersecurity solutions.

He set out to build an organization that listens first, understands every customer's unique challenges, and stands beside them through every stage of their cybersecurity journey.

From day one, MMA Infosec's purpose has been clear: to earn trust through expertise, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to customer success.

Our Story

Building a Trusted Partner

"We believe the strongest partnerships are built by understanding challenges before solving them."

Every organization faces challenges. Some are operational, some are strategic, and today, many are digital.

At MMA, our customer-first philosophy drives everything we do — from the way we engage with clients to the way we design and deliver cybersecurity solutions.

What started as a vision in 2020 has rapidly evolved into one of UAE's most trusted cybersecurity partners, serving organizations across the country.

Today the company is headquartered in UAE and also has its extended arms in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain and India.

Our Vision

In Pursuit of Excellence

Excellence is not a destination — it is our way of thinking. It shapes how we innovate, how we serve our customers, how we develop our people, and how we continuously raise the standard of cybersecurity excellence.

Our Purpose

Helping Customers Conquer Their Goliaths

The story of David and Goliath is more relevant today than ever. Every organization faces its own Goliaths — whether they are evolving cyber threats, business challenges, or an increasingly complex digital landscape.

At MMA Infosec, we embrace the spirit of David. As a fast-growing company competing alongside long-established global organizations, we believe that success is driven not by size, but by courage, expertise, innovation, and an unwavering pursuit of excellence.

We extend that same mindset to every customer we serve. Our mission is to help organizations build cyber resilience, overcome their greatest challenges, and confidently face the future.
