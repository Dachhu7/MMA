Transparency & Trust

Privacy Notice

MMA InfoSec is committed to transparency about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. This Privacy Notice describes our data practices in clear, straightforward language.

1. Notice at Collection

At the time of collection, we provide individuals with clear notice regarding the categories of personal information we collect and the purposes for which the information will be used. This notice is provided at or before the point of data collection.

We collect personal data only to the extent reasonably necessary to deliver enterprise cybersecurity services, respond to inquiries, and maintain secure, reliable operations.

Transparency is foundational to trust. We will never collect your data without a clear purpose.

2. Categories of Personal Data

The categories of personal information we may collect include:

  • Identifiers: Full name, email address, phone number, postal address, IP address
  • Professional information: Job title, company name, industry, work history
  • Commercial information: Products or services purchased, billing details
  • Technical data: Browser type, device information, operating system, log data
  • Communications: Records of inquiries, support tickets, and correspondence
  • Inferences: Preferences, security interests, and engagement patterns

We do not collect sensitive personal information unless voluntarily provided by you in the context of a specific inquiry or engagement.

3. How We Collect Data

We collect personal data through the following channels:

  • Direct interactions: Forms on our website, email communications, phone calls, and meetings
  • Automated technologies: Cookies, analytics tools, server logs, and session recordings
  • Third-party sources: Public databases, business partners, and event registration platforms
  • Service delivery: Security assessments, onboarding documents, and support interactions

Where we receive data from third parties, we verify that such parties have obtained consent or are otherwise legally permitted to share the information with us.

4. Business Purpose for Collection

We use collected personal data for the following business purposes:

  • Delivering and managing cybersecurity services and solutions
  • Responding to inquiries, requests, and support needs
  • Improving platform performance, security, and user experience
  • Marketing and communications with your consent or legitimate interest
  • Compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
  • Fraud prevention, threat detection, and security monitoring

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Any sharing of data is conducted under strict contractual safeguards.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share personal data with the following categories of recipients:

  • Service providers: Cloud infrastructure, analytics, CRM, and security tool vendors
  • Professional advisors: Legal, accounting, and compliance consultants
  • Regulatory authorities: When required by applicable law or legal process
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

All third-party recipients are subject to data processing agreements and contractual obligations to maintain confidentiality and security.

6. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to know: Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of data collected
  • Right to access: Obtain a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions
  • Right to opt-out: Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Right to nondiscrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights

To exercise your rights, contact us using the information in the Contact section below. We will respond to verified requests within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Updates to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. When material changes are made, we will update the effective date at the top of this notice and, where appropriate, provide additional notice such as a site banner or email notification.

We encourage you to review this notice periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
