Privacy Policy

MMA InfoSec is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining high standards of data security, governance, and transparency. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we process it, and your rights relating to that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and organizational information necessary to deliver enterprise cybersecurity services effectively.

  • Full name, company name, and job title
  • Email address and contact details
  • Billing and contractual information
  • Support communication records
  • Technical logs and system metadata

We may also collect technical data including IP address, device type, browser version, geographic region, and behavioral analytics to improve platform performance, threat detection, and user experience.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under applicable data protection regulations, including GDPR and other global privacy frameworks.

  • Contractual necessity
  • Legitimate business interests
  • Legal obligations
  • User consent (where applicable)

3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used to deliver cybersecurity services, respond to inquiries, improve our platform, and enhance threat detection capabilities. We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

  • Deliver managed detection & response services
  • Enhance AI-powered threat detection systems
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Improve product performance and analytics
  • Maintain compliance and regulatory reporting

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement encryption, access controls, monitoring systems, and governance policies to safeguard sensitive information.

  • End-to-end encryption (in transit and at rest)
  • Role-based access controls and least privilege
  • Continuous security monitoring and SIEM integration
  • Zero-trust design principles
  • Regular vulnerability assessments and patching
Security is embedded into our architecture — not added as an afterthought.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with trusted service providers or regulatory authorities when legally required or necessary for service delivery.

When we share data with third parties, we ensure appropriate contractual protections (data processing agreements) and technical safeguards are in place.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill contractual obligations, legal requirements, or legitimate business needs.

Retention periods differ by data type (for example, billing records may be kept longer for tax compliance).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data.

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (Right to be Forgotten)
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality, improve analytics, and strengthen security monitoring. You can control cookies via your browser settings or our cookie controls where provided.

9. International Transfers

We operate globally. When personal data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, approved transfer mechanisms, or other lawful transfer tools to protect your data.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in regulations or operational practices. When material changes occur we will provide clear notice; otherwise, the updated date will be posted.

GDPR & Regional Notes

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or other jurisdictions with comprehensive data protection laws, you may have additional rights. We process data in accordance with applicable regional requirements.
